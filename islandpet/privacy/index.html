Privacy

Your island stays on your iPhone.

Island Pet: Pixel Diary Isle is made by Arthur Zheng. It does not collect, send, or share any personal information.

Last updated November 23, 2026

The short version

There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything you make in Island Pet is saved on your device, and nothing is sent to a server.

What Island Pet saves

To keep your game, the app saves the following in its own storage on your device:

  • your island and everything on it, including buildings, trees, villagers and their names, the names you give your island and your pilot, your level, and the resources you have gathered
  • the diary entries you write, with the mood and date that go with them
  • letters and postcards from your villagers, including small pictures of your island that the app draws
  • your settings, such as sound and music on or off

What Island Pet does not collect

  • No account or sign-in, and no name, email address, or phone number
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or third-party tracking tools
  • No in-app purchases

The app makes no network requests while you play. Its fonts and artwork are built into the app.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include Island Pet's saved data in that backup. Those backups are handled by Apple under your Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy, not by Island Pet.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your device removes everything it saved. The developer holds no copy, so there is nothing else to delete.

Children's privacy

Island Pet is rated 4+. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Island Pet changes how it handles information, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will be revised.
