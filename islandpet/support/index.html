Where is my island saved?

Island Pet saves your island, diary entries, and letters on your iPhone, inside the app. There is no account and nothing is stored online.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Island Pet works fully offline.

Can I move my island to a new iPhone?

Island Pet does not sync between devices. When you move to a new iPhone by restoring an iCloud or computer backup, your island may come along with the rest of your app data.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting Island Pet removes your island and diary from the device. There is no copy anywhere else, so it cannot be recovered unless it is in a device backup.

Are there ads or purchases?

No. Island Pet has no ads and no in-app purchases.
